# Sample images

`IDRiD_*.jpg` are from the official **test** split of the Indian Diabetic Retinopathy Image Dataset
(IDRiD), Segmentation subset. None of them was used to train or tune any NETRA model.

> Prasanna Porwal, Samiksha Pachade, Ravi Kamble, Manesh Kokare, Girish Deshmukh, Vivek Sahasrabuddhe,
> Fabrice Meriaudeau. "Indian Diabetic Retinopathy Image Dataset (IDRiD)", IEEE Dataport, 2018.
> doi:10.21227/H25W98
